The 4th Annual Swede Sunday is coming up! Come hang out with fellow enthusiasts who love their Volvos and Swedish cars! Whether you’ve got a pristine show car, a project that’s still a work in progress, or just an appreciation for all things Swedish, you’re welcome here. We’ll kick things off with our initial car show, giving everyone plenty of time to walk around, check out the cars, and meet other enthusiasts. Once the show wraps up, we’ll hit the road for a cruise through some of the local back roads, enjoying the cars and the scenery together.
